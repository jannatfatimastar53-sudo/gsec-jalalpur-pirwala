General Overview & Mandate

A Beacon of Inclusive Learning in Jalalpur Pirwala

Established under the Special Education Department, Government of Punjab, the centre delivers free, equitable, and modern education designed specifically for differently-abled children in Tehsil Jalalpur Pirwala and surrounding regions of District Multan.

Our pedagogical approach combines formal academic curricula with intensive clinical therapies, psychological counseling, and life-skills training to help every student achieve their maximum cognitive, physical, and vocational potential.

Child-Centric Growth

Individualized Education Plans (IEPs) tailored to each child’s unique pace and abilities.

Free Safe Transport

Equipped school buses ensuring comfortable, monitored pick-and-drop to all students.

The campus features dedicated, trained faculty (JSET & SSET), specialized allied health professionals, modern therapy facilities, and a secure environment supervised by institutional safety protocols.

Punjab Special Education

Certified curriculum, psychological milestones, and state-backed inclusive welfare.

Academic & Clinical Wings

Specialized Categories Served

Four dedicated wings equipped with adaptive learning aids, therapy rooms, and customized teaching methodologies.

HI Wing

Hearing Impaired (HI)

Complete communication development through Pakistan Sign Language (PSL), lip-reading, speech articulation training, and audiometric evaluations.

  • Speech & Language Therapy
  • Auditory Verbal Stimulation
  • Supervised by SSET (BS-17)
PD Wing

Physically Disabled (PD)

Motor rehabilitation, mobility guidance, and accessible classrooms tailored for children with cerebral palsy, orthopedic impairments, and muscular conditions.

  • Physical & Occupational Therapy
  • Wheelchair Ramps & Accessibility
  • Adaptive Seating & Furniture
VI Wing

Visually Impaired (VI)

Braille literacy, tactile math, orientation & mobility training, and auditory digital learning tools to empower visually challenged learners.

  • Braille Reading & Writing
  • Cane Mobility Orientation
  • Tactile Learning Resources
Slow Learners

Slow Learners

Clinical psychological assessments, personalized pace modules, behavioral therapies, and cognitive exercises managed under M.Phil Psychological guidance.

  • Individual Education Plans (IEP)
  • Behavioral Therapy Modules
  • Cognitive Growth Tracking
