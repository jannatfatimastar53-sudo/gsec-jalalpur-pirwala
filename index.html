Mandate & Educational Role

Dedicated Special Education for Tehsil Jalalpur Pirwala

Established under the Special Education Department, Government of Punjab, Govt. Special Education Centre, Jalalpur Pirwala functions as the primary public sector educational institution for children with diverse physical, sensory, and learning needs in southern District Multan.

Our institutional mission is centered on empowering each child to achieve personal autonomy, functional communication, and academic development. We combine government-approved curricula with specialized instructional tools, individualized education plans (IEPs), and supportive therapeutic practices.

Individualized Education Plans (IEPs)

Custom learning goals adapted to the unique pace and developmental profile of each student.

Accessible & Barrier-Free Infrastructure

Equipped with wheelchair ramps, tactile pathways, and safety-compliant learning spaces.

Government Welfare & Material Provision

Tuition-free learning, textbooks, and subsidized transport as governed by departmental policies.*

“Every child possesses inherent potential. Our institutional role is to nurture that potential with professional skill, patience, and dignified care.”

Special Education Wings

Four Specialized Academic Categories

Our instructional structure is organized into four wings tailored to meet specific sensory, physical, and cognitive requirements.

HI Wing

Hearing Impaired (HI) Wing

Structured communication development combining Pakistan Sign Language (PSL), auditory-verbal exercises, speech articulation practice, and standard curriculum adaptations.

  • Pakistan Sign Language (PSL) Curricula
  • Speech & Language Stimulation
  • Supervised by SSET (BS-17) Faculty
PD Wing

Physically Disabled (PD) Wing

Specialized learning environments with barrier-free ramp access, adaptive furniture, fine motor development exercises, and tailored physical therapy guidance.

  • Physical Mobility & Motor Exercise Support
  • Wheelchair-Accessible Campus Grounds
  • Ergonomic & Adaptive Seating
VI Wing

Visually Impaired (VI) Wing

Braille literacy training, tactile learning resources, orientation and cane mobility guidance, and audio-assisted educational aids.

  • Braille Reading & Writing Instruction
  • Orientation & Cane Mobility Training
  • Tactile Mathematics & Geometry Tools
Slow Learners

Slow Learners Wing

Personalized pace modules, diagnostic psychological evaluations, individualized education plans (IEPs), and behavioral reinforcement strategies.

  • Individualized Education Plans (IEPs)
  • Cognitive & Behavioral Growth Monitoring
  • Structured Daily Life Skills Guidance
