# Govt. Special Education Centre, Jalalpur Pirwala - Official Portal

Official production portal for **Govt. Special Education Centre, Jalalpur Pirwala**, operating under the **Special Education Department, Government of Punjab (Public Sector)**.

## 🌟 Institutional Overview & Features
- **4 Specialized Academic Wings**: Detailed programs for Hearing Impaired (HI), Physically Disabled (PD), Visually Impaired (VI), and Slow Learners.
- **Universal Accessibility Suite (WCAG 2.2 AA Standard)**:
  - **Text-to-Speech (TTS)**: Web Speech API voice reader for institutional summary.
  - **High-Contrast Theme**: High visibility yellow/black theme with AAA contrast ratios.
  - **Text Sizing Controls**: Stepwise font scaling (`A-`, `A`, `A+`) with `localStorage` persistence.
  - **Dyslexia-Friendly Mode**: Accessible typography with enhanced letter-spacing and line height.
  - **Skip-to-Content & Focus Rings**: Built for screen readers and full keyboard navigability.
- **Institutional Leadership & Faculty**:
  - **Mr. Salman Hassan Ghouri**: Headmaster, SSET (HI / BS-17).
  - **Mr. Muhammad Tayyab**: Senior Special Education Teacher (SSET / BS-17) specializing in Hearing Impairment.
  - Statutory governance by the **District Education Officer (DEO) Special Education, Multan** and **Special Education Department, Government of Punjab**.
- **Parent Portal & Admissions UX**:
  - **Admission Inquiry Form**: Client-side validated form generating an official printable **Inquiry Reference Slip** (`INQ-JPP-XXXXXX`) for campus evaluation visits.
  - **Wing & Therapy Matcher Quiz**: Interactive guide to assist parents in identifying suitable specializations.
- **Campus Facilities & Transport**:
  - Information on textbooks, clinical assessments, student stipends, and dedicated bus routes across Tehsil Jalalpur Pirwala.
- **Campus Photo Gallery & Lightbox**:
  - High-resolution authentic campus photographs with full keyboard/touch navigation.
- **Technical SEO & Schema.org**:
  - Complete OpenGraph, Twitter metadata, and `EducationalOrganization` JSON-LD structured data.

## 🚀 How to Run
1. Open `index.html` directly in any modern browser (Chrome, Edge, Firefox, Safari).
2. Double-click `launch_website.bat` on Windows.
3. Deploy to any static hosting provider (Vercel, Netlify, GitHub Pages, Apache, Nginx).
